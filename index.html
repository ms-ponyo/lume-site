The web, read calmly.

LUME is a web browser for iPhone. A quiet reader, natural voices that read articles aloud, your mail and calendar in one calm place, and an assistant that only works where you let it.

Join the beta on TestFlight Free. Requires iOS 27. LUME for Mac is in beta testing too.

A reader that gets out of the way.

Open any article in a clean reading view. Choose Paper, Night or match your system, set the text size and spacing, and jump around with an outline of the page. PDFs and EPUB books open in the same reader.

  • Tables, quotes and code keep their shape
  • Picks up where you stopped reading
  • Translate with Apple's built-in translation
  • A Shelf for your books, saved articles and playlists

Listen to anything you'd read.

Turn an article into audio with natural voices that run on your iPhone. Download a voice pack once and it works offline.

  • Speed control and lock-screen controls
  • Two voice packs: 8 or 20 English voices, and 8 in Mandarin
  • Other languages use your iPhone's own voices
  • A queue, a transcript, and Talk: ask LUME out loud while you listen

Your day, in one place.

Your Gmail and Google Calendar, read from their own websites where you sign in, and the pages you follow, gathered calmly.

Mail

Read and answer your Gmail, with drafts and an outbox, and ask questions about your mail.

Calendar

Today, Week and Month views, new events, and your free times.

Updates and the brief

Changes on the pages you watch, and a morning and evening brief you can read or hear in two voices.

Passwords

Saved accounts, cards and addresses in your Keychain, filled with Face ID, and sign-in codes found in your mail.

LUME for Mac

The same browser with a tab sidebar, and it can share its Claude with your iPhone.

Memory you control

See what the assistant is told about you, and make it forget everything.

Help when you ask for it.

LUME's assistant stays out of the way until you call on it. It uses the AI provider you choose with your own key, or the Claude on your own Mac. Features that read a page ask you to turn on page sharing first.

Ask about a page

Ask a question about the page you're on and see the exact passages the answer comes from.

Summaries

Get the gist of a long article in one tap.

Compare tabs

Compare the tabs you pick in one written answer, with links back to the pages it read.

Watch a page

Tell LUME what you care about on a page, and get notified when it changes. Changes gather in Updates.

Hand over a tab

Let the assistant work in a tab you choose. A banner lets you stop it or take the tab back at any time.

Never in private tabs

AI features are switched off in private tabs, so nothing from them is sent.

Everything a browser should do.

The everyday things work the way you expect.

Tabs and tab groups

Pin tabs, sort them into named groups, and search across everything you have open.

Clean pages

Hides the ads and cookie banners it detects, and tells you how many. When hiding isn't enough, it picks the most private cookie choice.

Private tabs

Separate cookies, no history, and the page is never sent to AI.

Bookmarks

Folders, plus import and export as a standard bookmarks file.

History

Find a page you saw last week, or bring back a tab you just closed.

Downloads

Files save to the Files app, with a list you can search.

Find in page

Search for words on any page.

Site permissions

Choose Ask or Block for each site's camera and microphone.

Search

Search Google from the address bar. Sponsored results are hidden, and one tap brings them back.

Private by design.

LUME has no servers and no LUME account.

Stays on your device

Tabs, history, mail you read and settings are stored on your device. Passwords stay in its Keychain. No analytics, no ads.

Your key, your provider

AI features use a provider you choose, or your own Mac. Your key stays in your device's Keychain.

Off in private tabs

Nothing from a private tab is sent to an AI model.
